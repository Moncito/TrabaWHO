# NOVA — title card plan

**Angle:** premium 3D title card. Light out of fog becomes a name.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, 10s, no audio (user spec overrides brag defaults of ~20s + music).
**Tone:** cinematic, restrained. Dark navy-black, gold metal, cool fog.

## Identity
- Background: #04050a → #0d1220 centre glow, vignette, fine grain.
- Fog: cool blue-grey (150,165,195), soft sprites at depth.
- Gold ramp: #fff6d6 / #f5d27a / #a8741e / #6b4410 / #e9c063 / #7a4e12.
- Type: Bahnschrift Bold, letters hand-spaced, 14-layer extrusion for depth.
